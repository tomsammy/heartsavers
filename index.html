About The College

State-of-the-Art Private Nursing Education

At a time, a vision was born – a vision to establish a college of nursing science, a beacon of hope and healing. Tiamiyu Idowu, a critical care general consultant and NGO practitioner, was the driving force behind this noble endeavor. Nurturing and empowering future generations of compassionate nurses is our core calling.

As the first private college of nursing science in Kwara State, Heartsavers offers accredited ND and HND in Nursing Sciences. Our curriculum integrates theoretical knowledge with deep clinical rotations, preparing you to succeed on the global healthcare stage.

Programs

Nursing Sciences Curriculum

Heartsavers offers comprehensive pathways equipping you with the hands-on confidence and qualifications required for modern hospitals.

ND/HND in Nursing Sciences

A comprehensive, multi-year theoretical and practical program covering Anatomy, Physiology, Pharmacology, Patient Care, and Public Health.

Admission Requirements

  • Possess requisite O'Level credits (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB) in science subjects.
  • Meet the required UTME cut-off marks as stipulated by regulatory bodies.
  • Satisfy the minimum age requirements for tertiary institutions in Nigeria.

Executive Health Trainings

Advanced certifications and short courses tailored for health practitioners seeking specialization or professional development.

Program Highlights

  • Intensive workshops focusing on Critical Care and NGO Health Advocacy.
  • Hands-on labs using advanced medical simulation centers.
  • Flexible schedules and payment options for working health professionals.
